# Changes

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-10) — ALPHA

- Activity settings: what you typed in a template's fields is kept when you switch to another
  template and back (for example Gallery, then Escape, then Gallery again keeps the gallery's
  stations).
- Room editor: a warning now lists stations and objects that stand on or inside the furniture of
  the surroundings, where students cannot reach or see them well, for example after the
  surroundings were changed.
- Live rooms: when a teacher makes the room size smaller than the number of people already in a
  room, the room list now explains that nobody can join until some of them leave.
- A student's position in the room is now also saved when the page is left or the tab is hidden,
  so coming back resumes where they really were, not up to 10 seconds earlier.
- Fixed: finishing the same attempt from two windows at once (or while Moodle was closing
  it automatically) could record the completion, grade and finish event twice. A second window now gets a
  message that the attempt is being finished elsewhere.
- The built-in viewer's progress panel border follows Moodle's colour scheme, including dark
  mode on Moodle 5.3.
